Basic Filters
  - Contrast adjustment - Stretch or compress the color range
  - Saturation control - Make colors more vivid or muted
  - Sepia tone - Vintage photo effect

  Edge & Detail
  - Edge detection (Sobel/Canny) - Highlight outlines, great for artistic effects
  - Sharpen - Opposite of blur, enhances details
  - Emboss - Creates a 3D raised effect

  Artistic Effects
  - Pixelate - Retro/mosaic look
  - Posterize - Reduce color palette for a pop-art effect
  - Dithering - Classic game-boy style patterns
  - Vignette - Darken edges for a cinematic look

  Color Manipulation
  - Hue rotation - Shift all colors around the color wheel
  - Color channel swap - Swap R/G/B channels for trippy effects
  - Threshold/binarize - Convert to pure black & white
  - Color isolation - Keep one color, greyscale the rest

  Advanced
  - Gaussian blur - Smoother blur than weighted average
  - Noise generation - Add film grain or static
  - Chromatic aberration - RGB channel offset for a glitch aesthetic
  - Image rotation/flip - Basic transforms
